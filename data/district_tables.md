## High Cascades district proxy results

Approximate proxy within the Rogue River portion of the Rogue River-Siskiyou National Forest (administrative forest code 610). Cone allocation and the height zone; boughs = foliage plus branch wood, cut to the bole (primary harvestable estimate); foliage_only = secondary tip-cutting quantity. Latest reporting year: 2019.

Standing: 184 domain plots and 182 plots with eligible target trees; 372719 nonreserved acres. Growth: 168 domain plots and 377312 nonreserved acres. Species plot counts overlap.

Growth intervals: 9.1–12 years. 10 active tree records lack usable previous links and are excluded from the affected components; net is an observed-component subtotal.

Totals are nearest green US short tons (annual totals are tons/year), per-acre values have three decimals, and percent SE has one decimal. * marks every unrounded SE above 100%: the estimate is not distinguishable from zero. NA percent SE at zero is undefined. CSVs retain full precision. Sampling SE omits model and proxy-boundary uncertainty.

Mortality and removals are positive losses, subtracted in net change; survivor loss is signed negative. Net = survivor accrual + survivor loss + ingrowth − mortality − removals. These changes do not establish an annual cutting allowance.
